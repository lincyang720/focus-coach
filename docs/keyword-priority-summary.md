# FocusCoach Keyword Priority Summary

## Primary targets

- `deep work timer`
- `ADHD focus training`
- `ADHD focus app` as a content/listicle angle
- `ADHD productivity tools` as a content/listicle angle

## Excluded for now

- `ADHD focus timer`

## Reasoning

`deep work timer` is the best immediate product-page target because it has the cleanest combination of search demand and weak SERP occupancy. `ADHD focus app` and `ADHD productivity tools` are better served by list-style content rather than a direct product page. `ADHD focus timer` is too small to justify priority compared with the other two.
